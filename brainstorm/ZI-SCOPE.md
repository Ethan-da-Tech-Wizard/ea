# EA ZI: Read, Understand, Guide

ZI uses Roman numeral one, in honor of Konrad Zuse. This is a living analysis,
not a claim that EA has been implemented. Archi displays the planning artifacts;
changes to EA's scope do not implement capabilities in EA or Archi.

The complete proposed inventory and no-new-features rule are in
[END-STATE.md](END-STATE.md). Every included ZI feature must work locally with
4 GB total system RAM. Slower operation or disclosed reduced display detail is
allowed; accuracy, target precision and privacy are not traded for speed. This
is a binding requirement, not a measured feasibility claim. Analysis remains open.

## Product Intent

A friendly, cyborg-inspired desktop companion helps a person understand the
software in front of them. It can find information, inspect authorized sources,
organize evidence, assemble a proposed workflow, preview it, and explain what
the user could do. It also performs internal assistance, including a free
graphing calculator. The user does the external work.
The user also confirmed an embedded Archi workspace: EA can create and revise
methodology flowcharts inside its own interface on request. Generation and repairs
have shared finite budgets; a drawn workflow is never an executable workflow in ZI.

ZI should respect the person, their computer, other people's privacy, and the
environment around them. It stays interruptible and does not demand attention,
steal focus, waste resources, manipulate the user, or hide what it remembers.

The user explicitly names **peace, love and respect for people and technology**
as core values. Make them concrete: patient non-shaming help, accessible controls,
bystander privacy, truthful limitations and respect for correction/mute/leave.
No forced cheerfulness, fake intimacy, ideological lectures or engagement pressure.
Direct concise support is valid. User-controlled tone cannot change factual content.

## Customization And Internal Creation

Confirmed direction: useful defaults plus deeply configurable capability modules,
named profiles, local providers, appearance, key bindings, output formats and
declarative advanced settings. F-33..35 in END-STATE.md explicitly add module
composition, code/text artifacts and screen-annotation layers during open analysis.
Exact options/defaults and positive 4 GB feasibility remain review items.

ZI can take initiative within an explicit bounded request: compose a chart, code
draft or a screen layer with text boxes/circles/arrows, then present it. It does
not ask about every token or stroke, and it does not infer a new request from its
own output. Stop, finite budgets, permission checks and source lifetimes still apply.
Annotations are EA-owned, calibrated, dismissible and click-through, not actions
inside another app. Code/config/test/patch output remains inert and unexecuted.
Explicit Copy or new-file export of eligible EA-owned results is allowed; automatic
paste, project-source overwrite and executing generated work remain outside ZI.

Standard/Minimal/Custom module choices are independent of privacy presets. All
supported settings have rich help, effective-value explanations and reversible
reviewed changes. Imports cannot carry grants, scripts or undeclared capabilities;
disabled modules do no background work. Core Stop/Hide All/Forget remain reachable.
Arbitrary post-freeze plugins are not a customization loophole; user-authored inert
templates and provider replacements must stay within approved contracts.

The user confirmed accessible dataset review/export and trained-adapter import,
with actual training in a separately launched self-hosted tool. ZI provides no
trainer-launch capability. Preferences, factual memory and weights remain distinct;
each has its own provenance, consent, correction and honest deletion limits.
See SC-34..38, S-58..70 and EA-CFG/INK/MAKE/LEARN/CARE acceptance contracts.

The [capability catalogue](CAPABILITIES.md) expands the calculator, pointing,
and other personal-assistant candidates. The [acceptance draft](ACCEPTANCE.md)
defines observable outcomes, negative cases, and proposed benchmark targets.
Voice, animation, and advanced personalization are optional end-state modules
with versioned contracts; the text-first core must work with them disabled.
Optional does not waive their 4 GB positive tests or defer their scope definition.
See [complex scenarios](SCENARIOS.md), [rich settings help](SETTINGS.md), and
the [primary-source research and feasibility plan](RESEARCH.md).

The installer, settings editor, memory manager, and user-requested EA exports
may write EA-owned data under the relevant data policy. Computing, plotting,
and explicitly saving EA workbooks are also internal services, not external
execution. That does not grant the conversational assistant
authority to modify other software. An explicitly user-driven installer is not
an autonomous execution feature.

The same distinction applies to embedded Archi: editing EA-owned diagram state is
allowed under F-32, while controlling a separate app or overwriting its project is
not newly authorized. Draft history, autosave, export and linked source content
remain subject to EA's retention and revocation rules. See END-STATE.md for the
no-runaway-generation contract; the embedded feature has not been implemented.

## Computer Research And Conversation

Confirmed: find relevant information across approved local locations, extract
readable excerpts/tables with precise source references, ask focused questions
when intent is ambiguous, and carry eligible context and corrections across turns.
"Every file" does not bypass permissions, unsupported formats, encryption or
resource limits. Fast and thorough modes report actual inspected coverage and
gaps. No hidden disk inventory, source-file modification or automatic next pass.

Resolve exact document identity, edition, rule/section number and table units.
For "rule 432 of 1113", ask what 1113 identifies instead of guessing. Questions
offer meaningful choices or free text, Skip and Cancel; selection alone is not
submission or consent. The proposed three-round cap does not renew task budgets.
Summaries and saved conversations inherit source permissions, expiry and deletion;
they are not model training and cannot retain collection-off observations.

The user confirmed **approved local files and visible apps/pages only**, including
sports statistics and updated rules. State identity, observation time and any
freshness gap. EA may ask the user to provide a current source, but never fetches,
refreshes or navigates a page. The visible app may itself use the internet; its
activity is not EA permission for a network request or hidden feed. Explicit
installer maintenance is separate from this disabled runtime network capability.
See SC-30..33, S-54..57 and EA-SEEK/EXTRACT/TALK/CURRENT acceptance contracts.

## Research-Led Refinements

R-01..08 in [END-STATE.md](END-STATE.md) make existing capabilities more precise:
typed inert output with independent reference checks, inspectable evidence cases,
selected chart patches that preserve user layout, and bounded declarative branch
checks with counterexamples. A verified reference is not automatically a true
claim; a logical result is not proof that a real workflow succeeds or terminates.

The same refinement set requires independent resource supervision, complete
owned-store deletion status, trusted explicit artifact maintenance and linked
specification/test evidence. No controller may silently elevate privileges,
disable protection, renew a generation budget or treat outside text as consent.
Prompt injection, memory exhaustion and residual data remain risks to test,
not problems solved by a promise that EA is all-knowing or harmless.

These are recommended pre-freeze contracts, not implemented features or approved
numeric defaults. The proposed formal guard subset and case presentation need
include/exclude review now. Every included refinement retains the 4 GB positive
test requirement. Research evidence is in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md), and
[REFINEMENTS.json](REFINEMENTS.json) supplies checked cross-references.

The strictest read-only preset disables even menu reveal. If we retain the
earlier idea of revealing menus, it needs a tested app/version/state contract
and a visible permission. Unknown menus are opened by the user. No general
click, keyboard injection, or default-action invocation exists in ZI.

## Aspirations Into Tests

| Aspiration | Honest, Testable Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Read everything in a blink | Show useful accessible evidence quickly; measure time to first useful result and completed coverage separately |
| Read an entire source if necessary | Stream authorized chunks, preserve provenance, track unread content, and request bounded continuation when limits are reached |
| Find anything on the computer | Search reviewed roots and supported content; disclose unknown/unread coverage and unsupported files; never claim an exhaustive instantaneous scan |
| Think with me and remember | Resolve follow-ups from eligible context, ask material questions, preserve corrections and source limits; no mind-reading or automatic training |
| See what the user sees | Cover explicitly granted windows/regions; expose unreadable or protected areas; never bypass OS permissions |
| Never harm a computer | No external execution in ZI; isolate parsers/workers; enforce resource budgets; test failures, cancellation, and memory pressure |
| No privacy risk | Minimize and contain risk: local default, no silent uploads, explicit scope, protected-source exclusions, no content logs, deletion tests |
| Detect fake links | Explain concrete signals and missing evidence; never certify a link safe from appearance or model confidence |
| Learn a personality | Funny, useful on-request help; reviewed per-user mannerisms and language controls, no social check-ins or blended private profiles; measure whether optional owner-scoped fine-tuning adds value |
| Free | Audit every shipped dependency/model/data license and provide an offline path without metered services |

There is no honest zero-risk guarantee. The OS, swap, backups, compromised
accounts, capture bugs, parser vulnerabilities, third-party extensions, and
mistaken user grants remain part of the threat model. Do not claim forensic
erasure or perfect detection of sensitive content.

### Companion And User Boundaries

Separate profiles only is confirmed. Reuse a common read-only base model, never
pool private conversations, phrases, opinions, examples or adapters into one
personality. Identify users through a tested account boundary, not their writing
style or the workspace-profile name. Unknown identity uses guest/session behavior;
switches invalidate old owner/session output and private context before new access.
Same-OS-account authentication/storage remains a design decision and freeze risk.

Respond to stated frustration with practical help and optional fitting humor.
Current no-jokes requests override learned style; profanity is separately chosen.
No social check-ins or emotional dependency tactics. Learning means reviewed,
editable preferences first, with source eligibility and deletion still enforced;
it is not automatic weight training or agreement with every opinion. The full
contract is in END-STATE.md, TONE/OWNER and SC-39, within the same 4 GB target.

## Wizard And Settings

The first run stays inactive until the user finishes setup. The wizard covers
resource detection, model source and integrity, local storage, source grants,
collection, recall, retention, personality, startup behavior, and a final
permission review. It tests itself with synthetic fixtures, not personal data.

Every wizard choice must remain reachable in settings afterward. Simple mode
uses toggles and presets. Advanced mode uses structured app/source/purpose
conditions with inspectable effective results, never arbitrary executable rules.

| Control | Initial Proposal | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background observation | Off | Confirmed optional capability; explicit scope, input types, purpose, lifetime, and visible status required |
| Collect observations | Off | Controls durable retention, not acquisition; approved help/background reads stay temporary when off |
| Pause acquisition | Off | When on, stop new screen/file/app acquisition and cancel affected active reads |
| Recall saved knowledge | On, separately controllable | Recall does not imply new collection |
| Software-use history | Off | App identity/version events only, and only when collection is allowed |
| Scope | User-selected app/file | Optional explicit broad read grant; never root/admin or external execution; no whole-home or all-monitor grant by default |
| Captures | Ask per task | Background capture requires a separate recurring-read grant; no implicit continuous screen recording |
| Raw image retention | Off | OCR does not require saving screenshots |
| Menu reveal | Off in the strict ZI preset | Optional verified presentation-only adapter; unknown actions stay unavailable |
| Network at runtime | Disabled, confirmed | Only approved local files and visible apps/pages; installer maintenance consent does not enable runtime network access |
| Training eligibility | Off | Separate purpose consent, never inferred from ordinary collection |
| Energy and resources | Low-RAM preset | Idle unloading, no busy polling, bounded CPU, cancellation, optional battery-saver constraints |
| Personality | Explicit preference | No hidden sensitive-trait inference or manipulative profiling |
| Proactive hints | Off; include/exclude before freeze | Separate proposed capability F-29; background observation does not grant interruptions |

Confirmed collection-off behavior: an explicitly requested inspection is allowed
under source permissions, in temporary memory. It is never retrospectively saved
when collection turns on. Saving an explicitly chosen UI setting is distinct
from retaining observed content.

Permissions, activation, retention, and external actions are separate controls.
Even with broad read permission, ZI stays idle until an explicit request or a
separately enabled observation policy. The user confirmed that background
observation must be available as an explicit opt-in. Its operating defaults,
consent lifetime, and resource limits remain proposals in CAPABILITIES.md.
Turning that mode off stops background reads; Pause all reading stops both
modes. Neither deletes saved knowledge. Typed calculation continues to work
when source acquisition is paused.

## Knowledge And Temporary Caches

Start with SQLite plus FTS, not a mandatory vector database. A control label,
error string, app version, and source path are naturally searchable facts.
Decide optional embeddings before freeze after measuring missed useful results
and memory cost. They are a predeclared option, not an open-ended feature slot.

Temporary context has byte limits, short lifetimes, source revisions, app/window
identity, policy/data generations, and a sticky persistence-eligibility flag.
It is invalidated on target changes, source changes, cancellation, revocation,
deletion, expiry, or memory pressure. No disk-backed prompt cache is enabled in
temporary-only mode. OS swap and crash-dump limitations must still be disclosed.

The memory manager needs searchable records, app/category/source filters,
provenance, current permissions, expiry, edit/correct, per-record delete,
per-app forget, forget everything, export preview, and deletion progress.
Deleting a source reaches summaries, search indexes, vectors, caches, owned
exports, and owned backups. External copies and trained weights need explicit
limitation reports. A summary must not become a hidden copy of forgotten data.

## Local Model And Free Use

Model selection is not settled. Qwen3-0.6B is a text-first benchmark candidate;
its published license is Apache-2.0. llama.cpp is MIT-licensed. These are useful
inputs to a distribution review, not a substitute for checking every bundled
artifact and notice. See the [model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B)
and [runtime license](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE).

4 GB means total RAM for the operating system, current applications, and EA.
Every included feature, including local voice and bounded advanced mathematics,
needs a positive test on that baseline. There is no required higher-RAM tier.
Measure peak RSS, system available memory, UI latency, model load time, first
useful evidence, answer correctness, and abstention. A file's quantized size is
not its runtime RAM use. No GPU is required; model-free evidence mode is a
first-class temporary fallback, not proof that a missing AI feature works.
Training weights is outside ZI; reviewed exports and compatible adapter inference
are within proposed F-23 and must fit the same baseline. A named realistic
foreground workload and CPU profile are still needed to define test conditions.

The optimization direction is staged work, not an always-resident stack of models.
Use structured/exact evidence and deterministic specialists first when they meet
the question, bounded OCR for missing visual evidence, and the local model for
interpretation/explanation. WORK details finite stage scheduling and release;
RESEARCH.md P-17 compares actual stage costs and quality-preserving configurations.
Its illustrative allocation split stays within the proposed 1 GiB EA ceiling.
Cache, decoder, loader and cross-stage copies count; cold and warm results are
measured separately. All feature and permission requirements remain unchanged.

The user reports existing dataset and LoRA tooling in KARL. We have not inspected
or modified it. Before freeze, the compatibility contract must establish the exact base model, tokenizer,
adapter format, runtime loading or merge method, quantization order, licenses,
dataset provenance, and deletion/rebuild procedure. Do not assume that an
adapter can be injected into any GGUF file interchangeably.

## Security Guidance

Link checks use a standard URL parser, IDNA handling, and a maintained public
suffix library/list. Show the actual destination when available. A mismatch,
confusable hostname, unusual scheme, or shortened URL can justify caution but
does not prove maliciousness. Local static checks do not reveal redirects or
current server behavior. ZI does not visit the link or upload it for analysis.

Antivirus is an existing specialist tool, not something the LLM replaces. ZI
explains verified user-run steps and reads authorized output. It never starts
a scan, updates signatures, quarantines, or deletes files. Even a completed
scan with no alerts does not prove an absence of malware. For future scanner
integration, start with the scanner's own documented interfaces, such as
[ClamAV scanning documentation](https://docs.clamav.net/manual/Usage/Scanning.html).

## ZII Architecture

Reserve an interface, not an enabled capability:

```text
ZI:
    ExplicitRequest -> Intent -> InternalCalculator/Tools -> User
    ExplicitRequest -> ScopedObserver -> Evidence -> Calculator/Explainer -> User
    ActionBroker = ABSENT

ZII execution edition:
    Planner -> ProposedAction -> IndependentPolicyBroker -> ScopedExecutor
    Result -> IndependentObservation -> GoalCheck -> NextProposal
```

Long-running delegated work requires an explicit goal, finite time/step/CPU/RAM
limits, scoped leases, risk-based confirmations, revocation, progress checks,
crash-safe checkpoints when retention is allowed, and visible partial effects.
The loop stops on completion, cancellation, expiry, denied actions, repeated
failure, no progress, or excessive resource use. It cannot approve its own
permissions. ZI cannot acquire execution by changing a personality setting.
ZI and ZII are the confirmed edition names; ZII implementation is out of scope now.

## Decisions Still Open

1. Choose the first Qt app and GTK app for the demo and known-answer tests.
2. Decide whether ZI ships only strict passive inspection or also the optional verified menu-reveal adapter.
3. Choose default durable retention durations and storage quota after privacy review.
4. Benchmark models and quantization on a real 4 GB system before choosing one.
5. Choose the app license and validate the full distribution manifest.
6. Define measured latency/energy/coverage targets rather than calling every read instantaneous.
7. Design the companion's actual visual identity after the capability analysis is stable.
8. Approve calculator modes/domains and prioritize the other assistant candidates.
9. Approve the compositor/scale test matrix and broad-read preset wording.
10. Approve background-observation scope presets, consent lifetime, event/cache budgets, and any separately proposed proactive hints.

Acceptance draft numbers are starting hypotheses. A named test configuration,
independent correctness oracle, and measured results are needed before approval;
Archi's tests do not verify EA's behavior.

The exit checklist in END-STATE.md is authoritative for phase sign-off. Missing
formats/languages, security/storage decisions, scope approvals and 4 GB feasibility
evidence keep analysis open. After sign-off, design/implementation may sequence
approved work but cannot add new features or silently reduce the contract.

## Analysis Acceptance

- Every ZI capability has an explicit source grant, failure path, cancellation behavior, and retention rule.
- Every flowchart shape denotes one operation or one predicate, with detail outside the label.
- No path from the ZI capability registry reaches an external executor.
- Collection-off, revocation-during-read, delete-during-answer, stale-source, and parser-failure cases have tests planned.
- Broad answers disclose coverage instead of pretending to be omniscient.
- Link guidance never labels an unverified URL safe, and scan guidance never runs the scanner.
- The future execution boundary is documented without implementing or enabling it now.
- Internal mathematics bypasses source observation for typed input and never treats model prose as a computed result.
- Pointing verifies target identity before geometry and hides markers on invalidation.
- Requirement IDs, flows, and test evidence remain linked and reviewable as the analysis evolves.
